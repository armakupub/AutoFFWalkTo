Auto Fast Forward Walk To

Automatically enables fast forward while your character walks to a destination.

How it works:
- Automatically speeds up (default: 2x) whenever your character walks to a destination
- This includes the Walk To keybind (default gameshortcut: Y or Z), but also right-click
  interactions that require your character to walk somewhere first (looting, opening doors, etc.)
- Speed resets to 1x when you arrive, encounter danger, or press WASD
- Clicking a new destination mid-walk keeps the fast forward active

Settings (Mod Options):
- Disabled — mod off
- >> — fast forward (default)
- >>> — faster forward
- >>>> — fastest forward

Compatibility:
- Singleplayer only
- Requires Build 42.15 or later
